FAQ

Good questions.

Everything creators usually ask before applying.

What does it cost to start?

Nothing. There is no upfront cost, no inventory and no subscription. You only ever share profit on sales you actually make, which is why there is zero risk on your side.

How much can I earn?

You keep up to 80% of profit. Your earnings scale with how well the offer lands and how far we can take it, which is exactly what we work on every month.

Do I have to build the offer?

No. We develop it with you, shaped by what your audience already asks for, then run the distribution, delivery and scaling behind it. Your job is your audience.

How big does my audience need to be?

Opivider is built for creators with an engaged audience. Reach helps, but trust is what converts. A follower count on its own is not what we look at.

How do I get paid?

You earn your profit share as sales come in. Apply and we will walk you through the details during onboarding.

Ready to turn your audience into monthly income?

Apply in a couple of minutes. If it is a fit, we build the offer, run the engine behind it and split the profit, up to 80% to you.
